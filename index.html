How it works

A script fetches every store listed on rakuten.com and joinhoney.com twice a day (about 5:17 and 17:17 UTC), records the rate each site shows, and keeps every change. Stores are matched across the two sites by name and web address.

Reading the numbers

Rakuten pays cash back as a percentage of the purchase, sometimes a flat dollar amount. Honey pays PayPal Rewards points; 100 points is about $1. “Up to” means the rate depends on the category. A store counts as boosted when its current rate is clearly above the rate the site itself showed before.

Spikes

A spike is a rate that jumped since the last check: crossed 10%, up 5 points or more, at least double the usual rate, or a new all-time high. Spikes are posted to a GitHub issue thread and to the RSS feed. Rates are public figures from each site and are not advice; always confirm on the store page before buying.
